CAD Design Engineer / Draughtsperson

ABTECH UK Ltd, United Kingdom | September 2025 – Present

As a CAD Design Engineer and Draughtsperson at ABTECH, I produce the technical drawings and CAD documentation that take electrical enclosure products from customer requirement to manufacture and installation. I translate engineering and client specifications into clear, accurate drawings, considering component interfaces, dimensions, fabrication methods and installation constraints, and work closely with engineering and design colleagues to resolve queries and refine documentation as each project develops.

About ABTECH

ABTECH designs and manufactures LV, MV and HV electrical enclosures, junction boxes, local control stations, connection accessories and LED lighting for heavy industrial environments and hazardous areas. With roots in the earliest days of offshore oil and gas, the company supplies stainless steel and GRP enclosures built to international hazardous-area standards such as ATEX and IECEx, serving oil and gas, petrochemical, power and other demanding industrial sectors worldwide.

Key Responsibilities

  • Produce and develop technical drawings and CAD documentation for engineering products and assemblies, supporting design development, manufacturing and installation requirements.
  • Translate engineering and customer requirements into clear, accurate drawings while considering component interfaces, dimensions, fabrication requirements and installation constraints.
  • Prepare general arrangement and detailed manufacturing drawings for bespoke and standard enclosure assemblies, including component and cable-entry layouts.
  • Work with engineering and design colleagues to resolve drawing and design queries and iterate technical documentation in response to project requirements.
  • Maintain drawing accuracy and revision control so that documentation supports customer approval, production and on-site installation.
  • Design with hazardous-area and industry requirements in mind, ensuring drawings reflect the specifications and standards products must meet.
