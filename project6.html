Pedal-Operated Bin (3D-Printed Prototype)

Project Overview

This 3D-printed prototype of a pedal-operated trash bin integrates mechanical design, motion study, and additive manufacturing to demonstrate an efficient and ergonomic waste management solution. The design ensures hands-free operation, optimizing both hygiene and usability.

Skills Demonstrated

  • Mechanical Design – Engineered for smooth pedal operation, linkage mechanisms, and structural stability.
  • 3D CAD Modeling – Designed in Fusion 360, ensuring precision, manufacturability, and optimized assembly.
  • Additive Manufacturing (3D Printing) – Utilized FDM printing to prototype a functional mechanical system.
  • Material Selection – PLA for prototyping; ABS, PETG, or metal-reinforced plastic for real-world applications.
  • Motion Study & Kinematic Analysis – Simulated in Fusion 360 to validate pedal movement, force distribution, and component interaction.
  • Design for Manufacturing (DFM) & Assembly (DFA) – Simplified part integration for efficient production and user-friendly assembly.

Motion Analysis & Mechanism Design

  • Linkage System: The pedal operates via a lever-arm mechanism, smoothly lifting the lid with minimal force.
  • Load Distribution: The foot pedal, hinges, and lid movement were analyzed to ensure long-term durability.
  • Friction & Wear Considerations: Parts were optimized to reduce mechanical wear and improve the lifespan of the product.
  • Tested Opening Angle: Ensures maximum lid clearance while keeping the center of gravity stable.

Challenges

  • Balancing Aesthetics & Functionality – The challenge was to keep the design visually appealing while ensuring structural strength.
  • Ensuring Smooth Pedal Action – Required precise hinge alignment and lever optimization to achieve the right force balance.
  • Material Limitations in 3D Printing – PLA and ABS have different flexural strengths, so reinforcement strategies were considered.

Key Learnings

  • Iterative Prototyping – Multiple design refinements improved mechanism efficiency and part strength.
  • Material Optimization – Layer orientation and print settings significantly affect component strength and durability.
  • DFM for Mass Production – The 3D-printed version helped identify potential molded plastic or metal alternatives for large-scale manufacturing.

Engineering Ethics & Sustainable Design

  • Eco-Friendly Approach – Designed with biodegradable PLA & recyclable materials to reduce environmental impact.
  • Optimized for Manufacturing – Simplified part count and assembly process for ease of production.
  • User-Centric Design – Focused on ergonomics, accessibility, and hygiene benefits for daily use.

Prototype Specifications

  • Technology Used: FDM 3D Printing
  • Material: PLA (Prototype), ABS/PETG (Future Versions)
  • Mechanism: Foot-Pedal Actuated Lid System
  • Additional Features: Removable Inner Bin, Anti-Slip Base, Stainless Steel Handle

Hydraulic Scissor Lift Table

Project Overview

This hydraulic scissor lift table is a 3D CAD assembly demonstrating a two-stage scissor linkage driven by a single hydraulic cylinder. The design converts the cylinder's linear stroke into vertical lift of the platform, keeping the top deck level throughout its travel. The project showcases linkage kinematics, actuator placement, and assembly design for material-handling and workshop lifting applications.

Skills Demonstrated

  • 3D CAD Modeling & Assembly – Modelled the platform, base frame, scissor arms, pins, and hydraulic cylinder as a fully constrained assembly.
  • Linkage Kinematics – Designed a double-stage scissor mechanism to achieve greater lift height from a compact base.
  • Motion Study – Animated the hydraulic cylinder stroke to verify the full raise-and-lower range of motion without interference.
  • Actuator Integration – Positioned the hydraulic cylinder diagonally between the linkage cross-members to drive the lift.
  • Design for Manufacturing (DFM) – Used simple plate, bar, and pin components suited to cutting, drilling, and welding.
  • Photorealistic Rendering – Produced rendered visuals to present the design.

Design Features

  • Two-Stage Scissor Linkage – Stacked cross-arm pairs multiply vertical travel while keeping the collapsed height low.
  • Hydraulic Cylinder Drive – A single diagonally mounted cylinder raises and lowers the platform.
  • Slotted Guide Frames – Slots in the top platform and base frame let the sliding ends of the arms travel as the lift extends.
  • Pinned Pivot Joints – Centre and end pivots connect the arms while allowing free rotation.
  • Level Platform – The symmetrical linkage keeps the top deck parallel to the base at every height.

Challenges

  • Cylinder Placement – Finding a mounting geometry that gives enough stroke and leverage, particularly when starting from the fully lowered position.
  • Assembly Constraints – Defining pivot and slider constraints so the multi-stage linkage moved correctly in the motion study.
  • Clearance & Interference – Making sure the arms, cylinder, and cross-bars did not collide anywhere in the range of motion.

Key Learnings

  • Scissor Mechanism Behaviour – Understood how arm length, stage count, and actuator position affect lift height and required force.
  • Motion Simulation – Used animation to validate mechanism behaviour before committing to detailed design.
  • Assembly Planning – Structured the model into sub-assemblies for easier modification and manufacture.

Engineering Ethics & Sustainable Design

  • Material Efficiency – Standard steel sections and plate keep fabrication simple and reduce waste.
  • Maintainability – Pinned joints allow arms and the cylinder to be removed and replaced.
  • Operator Safety – Considered stable base footprint and controlled lowering for safe use.

Prototype Specifications

  • Technology Used: 3D CAD Modeling, Assembly, Motion Study & Rendering
  • Material: Mild Steel (frames and arms), Hardened Steel (pivot pins)
  • Mechanism: Two-Stage Scissor Linkage with Single Hydraulic Cylinder Actuation
  • Primary Use Case: Material Handling, Workshop Lifting, Load Positioning
