About CMD

CMD Ltd is a British manufacturer of power, data and cable management solutions for modern workspaces, founded in 1984 and headquartered in Rotherham, South Yorkshire. Its range includes floor boxes, screed and slab boxes, underfloor busbar power distribution, desk power modules and power hubs, cable management systems, and ergonomic products such as monitor arms. CMD’s products are specified for offices, education, healthcare and commercial fit-outs across the UK and internationally.
