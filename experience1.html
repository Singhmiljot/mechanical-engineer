About Clarivate

Clarivate is a global information services and analytics company, formed in 2016 from the former Intellectual Property & Science business of Thomson Reuters. It serves academia and government, intellectual property and life sciences, and is known for its Derwent patent products, including the Derwent World Patents Index, a curated database of patent applications and grants from patent authorities worldwide. Its IP services help organisations search, analyse, protect and maintain their patents and trademarks.
