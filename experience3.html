About Ather Energy

Ather Energy is an Indian electric two-wheeler manufacturer headquartered in Bengaluru, founded in 2013. It designs its electric scooters, including the Ather 450 series and the family-focused Rizta, largely in-house, along with their battery packs, software and the Ather Grid fast-charging network. Its vehicles and battery packs are assembled at its manufacturing facility in Hosur, Tamil Nadu.
