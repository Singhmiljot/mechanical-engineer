About Me

I'm a Mechanical Design Engineer based in the United Kingdom, currently working as a CAD Design Engineer / Draughtsperson at ABTECH, where I produce technical drawings and CAD documentation for hazardous-area electrical enclosures used in oil and gas and heavy industry.

I hold an MSc in Advanced Mechanical Engineering from the University of Sheffield and a BTech in Mechanical Engineering (Automobile) from Dehradun Institute of Technology. My work combines CAD modelling, precision drawings and FEA/CFD analysis to turn requirements into robust, manufacturable designs.

What I bring

  • Drawings that build right first time: clear GA, detail and assembly drawings with GD&T, tolerances and BOMs, prepared for manufacture and installation.
  • Analysis-led design: FEA and CFD in ANSYS to check strength and performance before anything is made.
  • Design for manufacture: DFM/DFA thinking from 3D printing and CNC prototypes through to fabricated and assembled products.
  • Cross-industry perspective: oil and gas, electric vehicles, medical devices, commercial power systems and patent analysis.
  • Team player: used to working with engineering, sales, QA and production teams to resolve queries and keep projects moving.
